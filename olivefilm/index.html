Olive Film

A calm film camera with natural looks and composition guides that help you frame every shot.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • Film-look filters

    Natural, soft film looks applied live in the camera and in the editor.

  • Adjustable effects

    Fine-tune brightness, contrast, warmth, grain, glow and more.

  • Composition guides

    Framing overlays and step-by-step guides to help you compose better photos.

  • Composition reels

    Short inspiration reels in the Home feed, bundled with the App.

  • Photo, video & Live Photo editing

    Re-edit photos, videos and Live Photos from your library.

  • Private by design

    Everything is processed on your device. No account, no uploads.
