Olive Film Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dreammers Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Olive Film application for iPhone ("App", bundle ID com.dreammers.olivefilm). We designed Olive Film to collect as little as possible.

1. Summary

  • Olive Film has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, or other contact details.
  • All photo and video processing happens on your device. Your photos, videos and Live Photos are never uploaded to us.
  • Olive Film shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We use Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, to keep the App working well.

2. Information We Collect

Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, collects usage events such as app opens, screens viewed and features used (for example, which filter was applied), together with an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, iOS version, language and approximate country or region. The app-instance identifier is randomly generated, is specific to this App installation, and is reset if you delete and reinstall the App. This data is not linked to your identity, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Ad personalization and IDFA collection are disabled.

Crash reports and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the App crashes or encounters a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and diagnostic information, such as the stack trace, device model, iOS version, app version, free memory and disk space, device orientation, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

Information that stays on your device

  • Photos, videos and Live Photos you capture or edit are processed locally and saved to your photo library. We never receive them.
  • Settings and preferences (such as favorite filters and camera options) are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect contacts, browsing history, precise location, health data, financial data, or any other sensitive data.

3. Device Permissions

The App asks for permission only when a feature needs it. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings → Olive Film.

  • Camera: to take photos, videos and Live Photos with live filters. The camera feed is processed on your device and is not recorded or transmitted except when you capture and save.
  • Microphone: to record sound for videos and Live Photos. Audio is saved only with the media you capture.
  • Photo library (add): to save the photos and videos you take or edit.
  • Photo library (read): to show your photos in the in-app album so you can edit them. You can grant full or limited access. Your library is never uploaded.
  • Location (optional, while using): only if you turn on the setting to save location info, the App writes the location where a photo was taken into that photo's metadata on your device. We never receive your location. Note that if you share such a photo, its location metadata goes with it.
  • Notifications (optional): to show App notifications. We do not use notifications for advertising, and the App does not collect a push token.

4. How We Use Information

  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the App.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and performance problems.
  • To keep the App secure and working as intended.

We do not sell or share your personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

5. Service Providers

Analytics and crash data are processed by Google LLC (Firebase) on our behalf, under Google's data processing terms. Data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States, with appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

  • Firebase Analytics: user-level event data is kept for Firebase's default retention period (currently 2 months) and then deleted. Aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash stack traces and related diagnostic data are kept for Crashlytics' default period (currently 90 days).
  • On-device data stays on your device until you delete it or delete the App.

7. Your Privacy Rights (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

Where the GDPR applies, we process analytics and crash data on the basis of our legitimate interests in maintaining and improving the App, and you may object at any time.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot link analytics or crash data to you by name. To make a request, email us at dreammers.studio@gmail.com. If needed, we will explain how to find your app-instance identifier so we can locate related data. You can also stop all collection at any time by deleting the App, which also resets its identifiers.

8. Children's Privacy

Olive Film is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not require an account, does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and contains no ads. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Your photos stay on your device and are protected by iOS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
