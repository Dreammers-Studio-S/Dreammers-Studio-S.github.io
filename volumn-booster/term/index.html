2. Description of Service

VolumnBooster is an audio controller utility for macOS. It offers output audio device switching, system volume controls, audio amplification (boosting system output up to 400% / +12 dB), and independent volume control for individual active applications (per-app mixing).

Some features are free, while others are designated as **Premium** (such as volume boosting past 120% and per-app volume adjustments) and require a paid in-app subscription or lifetime purchase. We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue any aspect of the App at any time.
