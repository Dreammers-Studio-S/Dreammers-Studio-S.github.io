YoungPhoto Support

Updated: October 21, 2026

Need help with YoungPhoto? Check the answers below. If you still need help, email us at dreammers.studio@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

The camera shows a black screen or asks for access

YoungPhoto needs Camera access to show the live preview. Open Settings → YoungPhoto and turn on Camera. If another app is using the camera, close it and reopen YoungPhoto.

Which permissions does YoungPhoto use, and why?

  • Camera: to take photos and Live Photos with live filters.
  • Photos: to save your shots, and to show your photos in the album so you can edit them. You can choose Limited Access and pick specific photos.
  • Location (optional): only used when you turn on saving location info, to add where a photo was taken to its metadata.
  • Notifications (optional): asked during onboarding for App notifications. You can decline.

You can change any permission at any time in Settings → YoungPhoto. YoungPhoto does not use the microphone. Your photos and videos are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Where are my photos saved?

Photos and Live Photos you take, and photos and videos you edit, are saved to your device's Photos library. If saving fails, make sure Photos access is allowed in Settings → YoungPhoto → Photos and that your device has enough free storage. Edits never overwrite your original photo; a new copy is saved.

I don't see all my photos in the album

If you granted Limited Access, only the photos you selected are visible. To change this, go to Settings → YoungPhoto → Photos and choose Full Access, or select more photos.

How do Live Photos work?

Turn on Live Photo in the camera to capture a short moving clip with your photo. The filter is applied to both the still image and the motion, and it is saved as a Live Photo in your library. Live Photos are saved without sound and need a device that supports Live Photos. YoungPhoto takes photos and Live Photos only; it does not record video.

How do I use filters and effects?

Pick a look in the camera's Filters tab and drag the slider to set its strength. Switch to Adjust to set exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth and tint, and to Frame to show one of 10 composition overlays with its step-by-step guide. The same filters and adjustments are available in the album editor for photos and videos already in your library.

Does YoungPhoto upload my photos or require an account?

No. There is no account and your photos and videos never leave your device unless you share them yourself. We only collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the App, and you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Share Analytics & Crash Reports. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off saving location?

Turn off Save Photo Location in the App's Settings, or set Location to Never in Settings → YoungPhoto → Location.

Something isn't working

  • Update to the latest version of YoungPhoto from the App Store.
  • Close and reopen the App, or restart your device.
  • Check that the needed permissions are allowed in Settings.

If the problem continues, email us with your device model, iOS version, App version and a short description of what happened.
