iPhone & iPad App

YoungPhoto

A calm film camera with natural looks and composition guides that help you frame every shot.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • Film-look filters

    11 natural, soft film looks (including Original) applied live in the camera and in the editor, each with adjustable strength.

  • Adjustments

    Fine-tune exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth and tint.

  • Composition guides

    10 framing overlays, each with a step-by-step guide, plus 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9 aspect ratios.

  • Composition reels

    Short inspiration reels in the Home feed, bundled with the App.

  • Live Photos & library editing

    Capture filtered Live Photos (no sound, on supported devices), and apply filters and adjustments to photos and videos from your library.

  • Private by design

    Photos and videos are processed on your device and never uploaded. No account, no ads. Anonymous analytics can be turned off in Settings.
