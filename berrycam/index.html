iPhone App

Berry Cam

A cute film camera with dozens of filters and recommended looks for photos, videos and Live Photos.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • 51+ film filters

    Swipe through a filter carousel or search the full library by category, and save favorites.

  • Recommended looks

    Each filter comes with suggested effect presets so you get a great look in one tap.

  • Photos, videos & Live Photos

    Shoot with live filters in 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9, with zoom, flash, grid and front camera.

  • Powerful editor

    Edit photos, videos and Live Photos with undo/redo and hold-to-compare.

  • Made for everyone

    Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support.

  • Private by design

    Everything is processed on your device. No account, no uploads.
