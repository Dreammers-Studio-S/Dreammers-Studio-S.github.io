Berry Film

Berry Film: Filter Style Cam

A sweet, soft film camera: 51 live filters, recommended looks and a simple editor for your photos, videos and Live Photos.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • 51 live film filters

    Signature, Selfie, Sweet and Mood looks (plus Original), previewed live in the camera with adjustable strength. Swipe to switch, search the full list and keep favorites.

  • Effects & recommended looks

    7 effects to fine-tune each shot, and recommended looks for every filter to get a great result in one tap.

  • A real camera

    1:1, 4:3 and 16:9, zoom, flash, front camera, tap to focus, grid, Live Photo capture, and the volume buttons as a shutter.

  • Editor

    Apply filters and effects to photos, videos and Live Photos from your library, with undo/redo and hold-to-compare with the original.

  • Album, save & share

    A built-in album to pick what to edit, then save a new copy to Photos and share it straight away. Originals are never overwritten.

  • Private by design

    Photos and videos are processed on your device and never uploaded. No account, no ads. Anonymous analytics can be turned off in Settings.

Available in English, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). Free to download, with optional Berry Film Premium.
