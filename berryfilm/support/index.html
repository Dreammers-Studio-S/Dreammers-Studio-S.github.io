Berry Film Support

Updated: October 21, 2026

Need help with Berry Film? Check the answers below. If you still need help, email us at dreammers.studio@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Premium screen in the App and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Berry Film Premium. Both the weekly subscription and the lifetime purchase can be restored on your iPhone and iPad.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, choose Berry Film and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or free trial) to avoid the next charge. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is free and what is Premium?

Free: Original plus 12 filters, 3 effects, their recommended looks, photo and Live Photo capture, and photo editing. Premium unlocks all 51 filters, all 7 effects, all recommended looks, and video and Live Photo editing. Locked items show a lock icon.

The camera shows a black screen or asks for access

Berry Film needs Camera access to show the live preview. Open Settings → Berry Film and turn on Camera. If another app is using the camera, close it and reopen Berry Film.

Which permissions does Berry Film use, and why?

  • Camera: to take photos and Live Photos with live filters.
  • Microphone: to record sound in Live Photos. Without it, Live Photos are saved silently.
  • Photos: to save your shots, and to show your photos and videos in the album so you can edit them. You can choose Limited Access and pick specific items.
  • Location (optional): only used when you turn on Save Location, to add where a photo was taken to its metadata.
  • Notifications (optional): you can decline.

You can change any permission at any time in Settings → Berry Film. Your photos and videos are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Where are my photos saved?

Photos and Live Photos you take, and photos and videos you edit, are saved to your device's Photos library. If saving fails, make sure Photos access is allowed in Settings → Berry Film → Photos and that your device has enough free storage. Edits never overwrite your original; a new copy is saved, and you can share it right away from the save screen.

I don't see all my photos in the album

If you granted Limited Access, only the items you selected are visible. To change this, go to Settings → Berry Film → Photos and choose Full Access, or select more photos.

How do Live Photos work?

Turn on Live Photo in the camera to capture a short moving clip with your photo. The filter is applied to both the still image and the motion, and it is saved as a Live Photo in your library (with sound if microphone access is allowed). Live Photo capture needs a device that supports it. You can also open Live Photos from your library in the editor.

How do I use filters, effects and the editor?

Swipe the carousel or open the full filter list (with search, categories and favorites) to pick a look, and drag the slider to set its strength. Try a recommended look for a ready-made combination, or switch to effects to fine-tune. In the editor, use undo/redo, and press and hold the photo to compare with the original. The volume buttons also work as a shutter while the camera is open.

Does Berry Film work on iPad?

Yes. Berry Film runs on iPhone and iPad with the same features, and Premium purchases can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Does Berry Film upload my photos or require an account?

No. There is no account and your photos and videos never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

How do I turn off analytics?

We only collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve the App, with no advertising identifier and no tracking. To turn this off, open the App's Settings → Privacy and turn off Share Analytics & Crash Reports. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off saving location?

Turn off Save Location in the App's Settings, or set Location to Never in Settings → Berry Film → Location.

Something isn't working

  • Update to the latest version of Berry Film from the App Store.
  • Close and reopen the App, or restart your device.
  • Check that the needed permissions are allowed in Settings.

If the problem continues, email us with your device model, iOS version, App version and a short description of what happened.
